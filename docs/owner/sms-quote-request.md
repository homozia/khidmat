# SMS quote request (send to 3–4 Pakistani SMS/OTP aggregators)

Send the same message to several providers so the quotes compare like for like. Ask each for a
written quote and a test account. Do not share any API keys or credentials in email.

---

**Subject:** Quote request: OTP / transactional SMS for a new services app (Islamabad–Rawalpindi)

Assalam-o-Alaikum,

We are launching a home-services booking app in Islamabad and Rawalpindi and need an SMS provider
for one-time passwords (log-in codes) and a few transactional updates (booking confirmed, provider on
the way). Please send a written quote covering the points below.

**Expected volume**
- Pilot (first 3 months): about 1,500–2,500 SMS per month
- Growth stage: about 12,000–20,000 SMS per month
- Messages are short (one segment, English); a small share in Urdu (Unicode)

**Please quote**
1. Price per SMS on your OTP / transactional route, for all networks (Jazz, Zong, Telenor, Ufone, SCOM),
   at both volumes. Is the price the same for every network?
2. Price per Urdu (Unicode) message, and how many characters fit in one segment.
3. Branded sender ID (masking): one-time fee, monthly fee if any, documents you need from us, and the
   usual approval time with the networks.
4. Any setup, monthly minimum, or prepaid top-up requirement; validity of purchased credit.
5. API: HTTPS/REST endpoint, authentication method, delivery reports (webhook or polling), rate limits.
6. Typical delivery time for OTP messages and your uptime commitment; what happens during a network outage.
7. PTA compliance: what you require from us (company documents, message templates, opt-out handling).
8. Whether you also offer WhatsApp authentication messages, and at what price.
9. A free test account or test credit so we can measure delivery speed before signing.

We plan to decide within two weeks of receiving quotes.

Regards,
[Your name]
[Company name, once registered]
[Phone] · [Email]

---

**How to compare the replies:** cost for 2,000 and 15,000 OTPs a month including masking fees; delivery
time measured with the test account on all four networks; and whether credit expires. Avoid
international API providers for OTP: their list prices to Pakistan were USD 0.26–0.47 per SMS
(about PKR 72–132) when checked in November 2026.
